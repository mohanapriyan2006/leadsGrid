# Legal Pages Feature

## Overview
Public legal pages that cover privacy and terms for LeadsGrid. These pages are optimized for clarity, scannability, and on-brand presentation.

## Components
- `LegalHero`
- `LegalSectionCard`
- `PrivacyContent`
- `TermsContent`

## Pages
- `/privacy`
- `/terms`

## Behavior
- Static content only.
- Uses `PublicPageShell` for shared navigation and footer.

## Future Improvements
- Add version history for policy changes.
- Provide printable view and export to PDF.
